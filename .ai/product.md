# Product

## Overview
PromptPilot is a prompt-driven content generation tool for creating, refining, and reusing marketing content across multiple channels from one workspace.

## Users
- Solo founders and small marketing teams
- Operators who need fast first drafts
- Teams that want reusable prompt templates without a heavy CMS

## Core Problem
Content teams waste time rewriting the same ideas for different formats and managing prompts across scattered docs and chats.

## Core Features (MVP)
- Create content from a reusable prompt template
- Enter brand, audience, and campaign inputs in one form
- Generate channel-specific drafts such as email, social, and landing page copy
- Edit and rerun outputs quickly
- Save a small library of prompt presets

## Non-Goals
- Full marketing automation
- Complex team permissions
- Deep analytics or reporting
- Large workflow builders

## Success Criteria
- A user can configure a prompt, generate useful multi-channel drafts, and iterate in minutes
- Prompt presets are easy to find and reuse
- The app feels fast, clear, and demo-ready with a minimal UI
